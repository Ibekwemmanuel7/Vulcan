# Mineral rights and permits roadmap

Status: planned records-linked extension of Vulcan. Existing prospectivity research remains separate. Portfolio hub: [Lillie US](https://github.com/Ibekwemmanuel7/Lillie_US).

## Proposed first decision

A mineral-management or environmental consultant needs to identify which portfolio records and observed activities need documentary or field review. Start with one customer-supplied portfolio, commodity and jurisdiction, not a claim of complete national title coverage.

## Separate entities

Keep surface parcels, mineral interests, private leases, state leases, federal claims, operating permits, environmental permits and easements as distinct record types. Do not infer one right from the existence of another. A parcel map is not a title opinion, and visible activity is not proof of lawful or unlawful operation.

## Records to design

Capture authoritative source, official identifier, jurisdiction, named parties as recorded, instrument type, commodity, source document and checksum, legal description, geometry source and uncertainty, recorded status, validity/review dates, retrieval date and known gaps. Retain version history, conflicting records and specialist adjudication. Restrict customer documents and assessments by tenant and role.

## Work packages

1. Agree the instrument types, authorised record access, commercial-use terms and customer question.
2. Build source adapters and a provenance-preserving document register; document incomplete coverage and delayed updates.
3. Georeference supplied descriptions through appropriate specialist review and preserve boundary uncertainty.
4. Link records and review dates without automatically treating apparent inactivity as expiry.
5. Compare independently observed surface changes against reviewed instrument geometry; flag uncertain overlaps for review.
6. Export a dated dossier with source documents, observations, uncertainties and review actions.

## Evaluation and limits

Use independently reviewed records to test field extraction, source freshness, entity linking, geometry agreement and review usefulness. Include missing, conflicting and superseded documents. Define customer acceptance and numerical targets before evaluating held-out records. Satellite prospectivity validation does not validate record interpretation.

The extension does not issue licences, certify ownership, replace title/legal/survey work, establish liability or calculate royalties without the necessary records and reviewed method. Jurisdiction-specific legal interpretation remains with the appropriate professional. User-specific rights and obligations are not determined by this roadmap.
