## Domain and status

**Domain:** Mineral prospectivity, mineral rights and permits

**Status:** Existing experimental prospectivity framework; rights and permit workflows are planned.

## Current baseline

The existing mineral-prospectivity code, tests and configurations are preserved. Current examples and scientific limits remain as documented; Southern US validity is not established.

## Planned scope

A jurisdiction-aware register of mineral rights, leases, claims and operating/environmental permits; document provenance, legal-description and geometry links, recorded status, review dates, and observed-activity comparisons. This requires records integration and specialist review, not satellite imagery alone.

## Ownership and interfaces

Keep surface ownership, mineral ownership, lease instruments, claims and operating authorisations distinct. A map overlap is a review candidate, not a legal finding. The platform does not issue permits, establish title, calculate royalties without the requisite records, or determine that observed activity is unlawful.

## Delivery gate

Define the buyer, decision, last useful delivery time, reference data, baseline and acceptance criteria before implementation. Outputs must carry observation and processing dates, provenance, quality, uncertainty and unavailable-data states. Customer-provided records and assessments require explicit access control. No new operational or scientific performance claim follows from adding this roadmap.

The repository owns its reusable domain code, methods, tests and domain documentation. Lillie US owns cross-product integration, shared customer workflows, portfolio configuration and product-release coordination. Do not make this repository depend on Lillie US merely to create a backlink.
